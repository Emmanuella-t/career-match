How Career Match works.

Three simple steps are enough for a landing page. Avoid clutter. Show a calm progression from input to insight to action.

1

Upload your resume

Give Career Match the context it needs to understand your background, strengths, and direction.

2

Match against roles

Analyze opportunities and identify which roles align most closely with your current profile.

3

Improve your next move

Use the insight to refine your positioning, strengthen weak areas, and apply more strategically.

Why this page style works.

The reference design works because it balances a bold headline, soft branding, and a friendly illustration without crowding the page. Preserve strong negative space and use the robot illustration only as a support element, not as decorative clutter.
